# Tables from the revised manuscript and supplement

The `main/` and `supplementary/` folders contain the exact table environments extracted from the final clean manuscript and supplement (9 and 14 tables). The index records their table numbers, captions, source pages, labels and hashes. Numerical entries are unchanged. Original snippets retain cross-reference and citation commands for reuse in their original document.

The two `*_tables.tex` files are standalone table-only reading documents. Compile with XeLaTeX. They preserve table text and data while replacing reference/citation commands with the numbers resolved in the corresponding final manuscript or supplement. Table layout is adapted to one table per page. Citation numbers refer to the references in the corresponding manuscript or supplement, not to a new bibliography.

These are publication tables, not independent simulation reruns. The experiment outputs and their protocol records are stored in the repository's supplementary experiment directories. No reviewer comments or response-letter content is included here.
